# Limitations

- This is an internal methodological investigation, not peer review or external validation. Leiden, DORA and OECD/JRC do not certify or endorse this model or its weights.
- The dataset is a partial, nonrandom collection of Sri Lankan profiles. It does not represent the full national academic population; results are not national estimates. The international benchmark is not the peer universe of the scored application panels, so it is excluded from these tests.
- Public records can contain duplicates, identity mismatches, incorrect metrics, stale employment, incomplete qualifications, heterogeneous years and provider-specific gaps. A platform link or accepted OpenAlex match does not independently verify the values. We count candidate duplicates but do not assert actual duplicates.
- The record-level analysis input is **pseudonymised, not anonymous**, so it is not published; only aggregate tables and figures are. This limits independent exact reproduction: the input must be requested from the maintainer. Small aggregate groups are suppressed in breakdown CSVs.
- Job titles do not measure career duration, time away, research allocation or discipline-specific career structures. Broad fields can hide major differences; unknown fields and stages fall back to heterogeneous groups. Titles, language and provider coverage introduce selection and fairness risks that have not been quantified causally.
- Available-case and complete-case analyses estimate different selected populations; restricting peers as well as subjects changes percentiles. Correlation is not evidence of interchangeable measures. Counts may refer to different document types, and source equality is not proof of matching dates or windows.
- Scenario contrasts are conditional on the captured dataset, chosen alternatives and selection. They are not confidence intervals, measurement error estimates, fairness certification or evidence of an optimal weighting. We did not perform a peer-sampling bootstrap or measurement-error model because the collection and error mechanisms are not established. We do not know if observed extremes are erroneous, and winsorisation is illustrative sensitivity only.
- No external outcome establishes the scientific quality, social value or reputation of individuals. There is no evidence here for hiring, funding, promotion, institutional league tables or individual performance assessment. Numerical correctness does not establish construct validity.
- The current application may present a 0–100 weighted component score as if it were an actual composite percentile; certain fallback wording can misstate a known stage. This study documents concerns without changing the UI or algorithm. Live scores remain subject to these limitations until separately reviewed.
