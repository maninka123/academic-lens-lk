# Ethics and data statement

## Status
Lanka Academia is an independent research project. It uses only publicly available professional and research information, and involves no surveys, interviews, or confidential records.

In October 2026, the Research Ethics Committee for Social Sciences and Humanities (RECSSH), Faculty of Arts, University of Colombo, confirmed in writing that it does not review work based on publicly available data intended for public use. The project has therefore had no formal ethics review, and no approval or institutional endorsement is claimed.

If the scope changes (for example, new data types, surveys, or contact with individuals), institutional advice will be sought first.

## Purpose
A descriptive, public-interest overview of academic capacity in Sri Lanka: who works where, how qualifications are distributed, and where research output sits relative to international benchmarks. It is **not** a performance review, ranking of people, or basis for any decision about an individual.

## Sources
- Official university staff directories and profile pages.
- Public research indexes (OpenAlex, and links to public Google Scholar and ResearchGate profiles).
- No logins, paywalls, private systems, or personal contacts. No CAPTCHA or rate-limit bypass.

## What is published, and what is not
- **Published:** an allowlist of professional fields (name as published, institution and unit, position, qualifications as published, research metrics with source and date, public profile links).
- **Not published on the website:** contact details, personal identifiers, family or private life, internal evidence logs, identity-matching notes and review files. Some internal files are still in the public repository ([DATA_POLICY.md](../DATA_POLICY.md)); move to private storage planned.
- Source links keep only the parameters needed to identify a public profile.

## Safeguards
- **Accuracy:** unknown values stay unknown; low-confidence matches are labelled "unverified" or left blank; groups below a minimum sample are not reported.
- **Context:** every comparison states its source, sample size and limits.
- **Individual figures:** shown only on a person's own profile, never as a ranked public list, and described as relative to collected profiles only.
- **Correction and removal:** see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). Requests are reviewed with a 14-day response target.
- **Provenance:** each value keeps its source and retrieval date in a private audit trail.

## Limitations
Partial coverage, possible errors and mismatches, and metrics that differ between providers. Presence or absence in the dataset says nothing about a person's merit.
