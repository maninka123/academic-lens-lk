# Google Scholar review — 12 October 2026

Searched **179 academics** by name, Google Scholar and university: 172 without a link and seven with links needing verification.

- **36 new verified links**; **four existing links verified**.
- **38 profiles have readable metrics**; two have a verified identity but no displayed metrics.
- **139 cases remain unresolved**. Broken links, conflicting accounts, wrong-person matches and possible duplicate records are retained for review.

[Full person-by-person review](../Data%20files/scholar_review_2026-10-12.csv) includes names, universities, outcomes and sources.

Only official named links plus a checked Scholar identity were accepted. All-time citations and h-index come from the public profile. The 2021–2025 window is added only when each annual count is displayed. Non-Scholar scalar metrics are preserved; new Scholar observations are recorded separately. Publication totals are not inferred from the first page of results. A failed search does not establish that someone lacks a Scholar account.
