SRI LANKA ACADEMIC ATLAS: PARTIAL COLLECTION
Collected 11 October 2026

Saegis Campus is excluded. The agreed scope contains 14 institutions.
This delivery does not complete an exhaustive census of every faculty and department.

This batch contains 1173 main staff records from 12 institutions, 1125 unique official source pages read, 154 official Scholar links, and 94 name-and-affiliation matched ResearchGate metric snapshots. Every main record received an individual research profile search. Only 79 records have a citation total; other matched snapshots may provide publication counts only.
The KIU and ICBT academic CSVs contain headers only. This does not imply those institutions have no academics.

FILES
Each institution has a separate academic CSV. academics_all.csv combines their main records.
coverage.csv reports extracted counts and gaps, not institutional staff totals.
faculty_department_inventory.csv reports discovered units and whether their links were visited. It is not a verified exhaustive hierarchy.
source_pages.csv records the official pages read.
research_metrics_snapshots.csv separates source-specific research totals.
research_search_log.csv records individual searches. Searches do not guarantee identity verification.
research_unresolved.csv lists unresolved research matches.
employment_review.csv keeps 77 records needing employment review outside the main dataset.
access_issues.csv records observed access issues. Interrupted discovery runs did not produce an exhaustive failed-URL log.
data_dictionary.csv explains fields and missing values.

LIMITATIONS
All main records remain automatically extracted and require factual review before publication or institutional comparison. Permanent or full-time status is not individually verified. Some directory pages may be outdated. Name variants or cross-institution appointments may remain duplicated.
Only 381 records have extracted qualification information. Blank values may reflect extraction gaps, unpublished information or inaccessible sources.
Google Scholar returned HTTP 429 on direct access. ResearchGate returned HTTP 403 on direct access. No bypass was attempted. ResearchGate values came from indexed search snapshots and may be stale. These counts must not be treated as Scholar counts or added to them.
All h-index, exact 2021 to 2025 citation totals, and Q1 publication counts remain blank. Q1 counts require publication-level matching with a consistent journal quartile source and publication-year/category rule.
A missing PhD record must not be counted as no PhD. Country mappings identify the institution's country, not necessarily the physical location of doctoral study.
Teaching experience is included only where explicitly reported. It can be approximate and source dated.
Discovery was interrupted after this partial batch. No collection continues automatically after delivery.

CSV FORMAT
UTF-8 with a BOM for convenient opening in Excel. One header row. Numeric counts are unformatted integers; empty cells are missing values. No invented example academics are included.
