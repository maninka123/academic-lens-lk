# Privacy and data-use notice

*Lanka Academia | Ongoing Research | Public Preview | Last updated 16 October 2026*

This notice explains what information Lanka Academia publishes, where it comes from, why it is used, how long it is kept, and how to contact us.

## 1. Who is responsible
Lanka Academia is an independent, personal research project run by Pasindu Ranasinghe. It is not an institution and has not undergone formal institutional ethics review (see [ETHICS_AND_DATA.md](ETHICS_AND_DATA.md)). The project is the "controller" of the compiled dataset. There is no company, office or staff.

## 2. What information we hold
About academics at Sri Lankan universities and higher-education campuses, **professional information only**:
- name as published, institution, faculty, department and position;
- qualifications as published, and doctorate details (awarding institution, country, year) where stated;
- research interests;
- public research-metric figures (publications, citations, h-index) with the source and date;
- links to the person's own public pages and research profiles;
- evidence status (for example "identity verified" or "unverified").

We do **not** collect or publish: contact details (email, phone), home address, date of birth, national identity numbers, family or private life, health, religion, ethnicity, political views, or any other sensitive personal data. A few derived indicators (for example a percentile "research standing") are computed from the figures above and are descriptive only.

## 3. Where it comes from
Only public sources: official university staff directories and profile pages, public research indexes (OpenAlex) and public Google Scholar or ResearchGate profile pages that the person or their university links to. We do not use logins, private systems or purchased data, and we do not bypass access limits. Some figures are read from public pages by hand. Each value keeps its source and retrieval date in a private audit trail.

## 4. Why we process it
To provide a descriptive, public-interest overview of academic capacity in Sri Lanka: who works where, how qualifications are distributed, and how research output compares between groups and with international benchmarks. It is **not** used to assess, rank, hire, promote, fund or discipline any individual, and no decision about a person is made automatically.

Our basis for this processing is the public-interest and research purpose of the project, using information already made public by the person or their institution, kept to the minimum needed. Requests to correct or remove a profile are considered as described in section 7.

## 5. Who sees it, and where it is stored
- The profile information is **published openly** on the website and in this repository, so anyone can read it.
- The project is hosted on GitHub (repository and GitHub Pages). GitHub processes technical data about visitors under its own privacy statement.
- Internal verification and investigation notes are kept privately by the maintainer and are not published.
- We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone beyond this public publication.

## 6. What the website stores about visitors
Lanka Academia has **no accounts, no analytics and no advertising**. Your browser stores a few settings locally on your own device (pinned academics, theme and that you accepted the first-visit notice). They never leave your browser and you can clear them at any time. A comparison "share link" contains only the selections you chose.

## 7. Contact and requests
Requests to correct or remove a profile can be sent through the routes below.

How to reach us:
- **Email:** pmranasinghe97@gmail.com
- **Public request:** https://github.com/maninka123/academic-lens-lk/issues/new/choose (use the "Correction or removal request" form; do not post private details)
- **Private request:** https://github.com/maninka123/academic-lens-lk/security/advisories/new

Our target is to respond to requests within 14 days. Where a removal is accepted, we aim to apply it in a later data release. Where a profile is removed, we keep only a minimal record that a request was made, so it is not re-added.

## 8. How long we keep information
- **Published profiles:** for as long as the project is active, or until you ask for removal.
- **Private audit trail** (sources, retrieval dates, matching notes): for as long as needed to check accuracy, and removed or reduced on request.
- **Old versions:** the repository keeps history, and copies may exist in caches, forks or archives we do not control. Where a request is accepted, we aim to remove the live data and, where practical, to clear earlier versions.
- If the project ends, the dataset will be archived without individual profiles or deleted.

## 9. Accuracy and safeguards
The data is partial and may be wrong, outdated or mis-matched, and it is not an assessment of anyone (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)). We use unverified labels, keep unknown values blank rather than guessing, apply minimum sample sizes, and show individual figures only on a person's own profile, never as ranked lists of people.

## 10. Changes
We will update this notice if how we collect or use information changes, and will change the date above. Questions about this notice can be raised through the contact routes in section 7.
