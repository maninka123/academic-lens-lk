# Privacy and data-use notice

*Lanka Academia | Ongoing Research | Public Preview | Last updated 22 November 2026*

This notice explains what information Lanka Academia publishes, where it comes from, why it is used, how long it is kept, and how to contact us.

## 1. Who is responsible
Lanka Academia is an independent, personal research project. It is not an institution. In October 2026 the Research Ethics Committee for Social Sciences and Humanities, Faculty of Arts, University of Colombo, was consulted and confirmed in writing that it does not review research based on publicly available data intended for public use. No formal ethics approval or institutional endorsement is claimed (see [ETHICS_AND_DATA.md](ETHICS_AND_DATA.md)).

## 2. What information we hold
About academics at Sri Lankan universities and higher-education campuses, **professional information only**:
- name as published, institution, faculty, department and position;
- qualifications as published, and doctorate details (awarding institution, country, year) where stated;
- research interests;
- public research-metric figures (publications, citations, h-index) with the source and date;
- links to the person's own public pages and research profiles;
- evidence status (for example "identity verified" or "unverified").

We do **not** collect or publish: contact details (email, phone), home address, date of birth, national identity numbers, family or private life, health, religion, ethnicity, political views, or any other sensitive personal data. A few derived indicators (for example the "research standing" score, a weighted blend of metric percentiles; see [STANDING_SCORE.md](../STANDING_SCORE.md)) are computed from the figures above and are descriptive only.

## 3. Where it comes from
Only public sources: official university staff directories and profile pages, public research indexes (OpenAlex) and public Google Scholar or ResearchGate profile pages that the person or their university links to. We do not use logins, private systems or purchased data, and we do not bypass access limits. Some figures are read from public pages by hand. Each value keeps its source and retrieval date in a private audit trail.

## 4. Why we process it
To provide a descriptive, public-interest overview of academic capacity in Sri Lanka: who works where, how qualifications are distributed, and how research output compares between groups and with international benchmarks. It is **not** used to assess, rank, hire, promote, fund or discipline any individual, and no decision about a person is made automatically.

We process publicly available professional and research information to support an independent research project examining Sri Lanka's academic landscape. We limit the information published to what is relevant to this purpose and provide procedures for correction and removal (section 7).

## 5. Who sees it, and where it is stored
- The profile information is **published openly** on the website and in this repository, so anyone can read it.
- The project is hosted on GitHub (repository and GitHub Pages). GitHub processes technical data about visitors under its own privacy statement.
- Internal verification notes are not shown on the website and are kept out of the current repository, in private local storage. Copies may remain in older Git history, in clones, forks and caches, and behind GitHub's own references (see [DATA_POLICY.md](../DATA_POLICY.md)); we cannot guarantee they are erased.
- We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone beyond this public publication.
- Cloudflare processes website analytics data for us under its own privacy terms (section 6). We do not receive visitor-level data.

## 6. What the website stores about visitors
Lanka Academia has **no accounts or advertising**. Browser preferences (theme, pinned academics and acknowledgement status) are stored locally on your device and never leave it. A comparison "share link" contains only the selections you chose.

### Website Usage Analytics

We use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to understand website traffic and improve the platform.

- **Privacy:** No tracking cookies, visitor identification or individual browsing histories. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected.
- **Data:** Cloudflare processes page addresses, referrers, approximate countries, device and browser information, and page-load performance. We access aggregate statistics only.
- **Visibility:** Only site-wide usage statistics may be published. Geographic and referral breakdowns remain private.
- **Limitations:** Analytics may miss some activity, do not measure unique visitors and cannot reliably distinguish navigation between pages.

These statistics describe website activity, not whether visitors read or believed particular information.

## 7. Contact and requests
Requests to correct or remove a profile can be sent through the routes below.

How to reach us:
- **Email (preferred, and private):** pmranasinghe97@gmail.com
- **Public request:** https://github.com/maninka123/academic-lens-lk/issues/new/choose (use the "Correction or removal request" form; do not post private details)
- **Alternative private route:** https://github.com/maninka123/academic-lens-lk/security/advisories/new, only if you cannot email (the form looks technical: [box-by-box guide with text to copy](https://github.com/maninka123/academic-lens-lk/blob/main/.github/SECURITY.md))

Academics may request correction or removal of their profiles, and no reason is needed for removal. Requests are acknowledged with a target response time of 14 days. After verifying that a removal request concerns the relevant person, we remove the profile from the actively maintained dataset and website and take steps to prevent republication. Earlier copies may remain outside our control. We keep only a minimal record that a request was made, so the profile is not re-added.

## 8. How long we keep information
- **Published profiles:** for as long as the project is active, or until you ask for removal.
- **Private audit trail** (sources, retrieval dates, matching notes): kept privately, off the public repository, for as long as needed to check accuracy, and removed or reduced on request.
- **Old versions:** the repository keeps history, and copies may exist in caches, forks or archives we do not control. Where a request is accepted, we aim to remove the live data and, where practical, to clear earlier versions.
- **Website analytics:** Cloudflare keeps the data for the period in its documentation. We keep dated, private, aggregate exports (counts by day, by country and by referring site, with no visitor identifiers) for as long as needed for research and to resolve disputes.
- If the project ends, the dataset will be archived without individual profiles or deleted.

## 9. Accuracy and safeguards
The data is partial and may be wrong, outdated or mis-matched, and it is not an assessment of anyone (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)). We use unverified labels, keep unknown values blank rather than guessing, apply minimum sample sizes, and show each figure with its source where available. Individual research metrics may appear in searchable and sortable academic directories and profiles. These figures describe recorded research activity and do not constitute rankings of academic quality, competence or professional suitability.

## 10. Changes
We will update this notice if how we collect or use information changes, and will change the date above. Questions about this notice can be raised through the contact routes in section 7.
