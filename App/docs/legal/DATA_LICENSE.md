# Licensing of data and published material

This project separates openly licensed research outputs from information about identifiable people.

| Material | Licence / terms |
|---|---|
| Project-owned source code | [MIT License](../../../LICENSE) |
| Original aggregate statistics and research reports (charts, summary tables, methodology text) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): reuse with credit, for example "Lanka Academia, https://github.com/maninka123/academic-lens-lk" |
| Individual academic profiles and records | Not covered by the project's Creative Commons licence. Applicable copyright, privacy and third-party rights may apply. |
| University photographs, logos, rankings (QS) and other third-party material | The original owners' rights and licences |
| OpenAlex data | Retains its original CC0 status |

Responsible-use guidance is advisory and does not impose additional restrictions on CC BY 4.0 materials. Nothing here grants rights over other people's information, publications or images. See the [notice](NOTICE.md) before relying on any figure.
